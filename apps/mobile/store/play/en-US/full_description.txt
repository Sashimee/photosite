Photoo connects people who need photos with photographers and professionals in Luxembourg.

FIND THE RIGHT PERSON
Browse photographers by category, language and location, look through their portfolios and read their offers. Categories include weddings, portraits, events, products, real estate and corporate work.

ASK FOR QUOTES
Describe what you need, with the date, place and purpose, and receive quotes from photographers. Compare them side by side and accept the one that suits you.

TALK DIRECTLY
Chat with photographers inside the app, share images and documents, and get notified when someone replies.

BOOK WITH CONFIDENCE
Pay securely in the app. Your payment is held until the work is delivered, and only then released to the photographer. Receipts are available in the app.

VERIFIED PHOTOGRAPHERS
Photographers can submit identity documents for verification, so you can see who has been checked.

FOR PHOTOGRAPHERS AND PROFESSIONALS
Set up your studio profile, upload your portfolio, define your products and prices, answer requests with quotes, manage your bookings and follow your payouts.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
You can download your data and delete your account from the Account tab at any time.

Photoo is available in English, French, German, Portuguese and Spanish.
