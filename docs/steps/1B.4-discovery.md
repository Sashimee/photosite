## Goal

Clients find photographers by place, category, language and price on `/{locale}/photographers`. Crawlable landing pages for country, city and category combinations serve SEO and ads.

## Decisions for this step

- **Routes.**
  - `/{locale}/photographers` is the search page. It's server-rendered from query params, so a filtered URL is shareable, and it's `noindex` whenever any filter param is present.
  - The landing routes are `/{locale}/photographers/{country}`, `/{locale}/photographers/{country}/{city}` and `/{locale}/photographers/{country}/{city}/{category}`.
  - Next.js allows one dynamic segment per level, so the landing routes live under the existing `[slug]` segment. A 2-letter lowercase segment is a country, because profile slugs are at least 3 characters (`SlugSchema`). `[slug]/page.tsx` dispatches:
    - a 2-letter segment renders the country landing, and 404s if the country isn't enabled in the `Country` table;
    - otherwise it renders the profile.
  - Nested `[slug]/[city]/page.tsx` and `[slug]/[city]/[category]/page.tsx` 404 unless `slug` is an enabled country, `city` is a known city slug for it and `category` is a `PHOTOGRAPHER_CATEGORIES` value.
  - No 2-letter slug can ever exist, and a test in `apps/api/src/modules/profiles/slug.test.ts` locks that in.
- **Cities.**
  - A new `GET /v1/cities?countryCode=&q=&limit=` returns `{ slug, name, countryCode, photographerCount }`. The data is distinct cities of published profiles, ordered by count, then name.
  - `q` is a case- and accent-insensitive prefix match with at most 60 chars, and `limit` is at most 20.
  - The slug is computed in SQL-free TypeScript with the same slugify rules as profile slugs, extracted to `packages/shared`.
  - The endpoint feeds both the city autocomplete and the landing page slug → name resolution.
  - It's public, cached with `Cache-Control: public, max-age=300`, and uses the default rate limits.
  - No new table: counts come from `PhotographerProfile`. A materialised city table waits until volume needs it.
- **Search filter.**
  - `GET /v1/photographers` gains `countryCode`. Contract first: regenerate openapi and api-client.
  - The existing `city` filter stays an exact case-insensitive name match. The landing page resolves the city slug to the name first.
- **Geolocation.**
  - A "near me" button uses `navigator.geolocation` only on click, never on page load. It sets `lat`/`lng` plus the default `radiusKm=25` in the URL, rounded to 2 decimals (about 1 km), so exact positions never land in URLs, logs or analytics.
  - No consent record is needed: the position is transient, user-initiated and isn't stored (COMPLIANCE).
- **Map.** Deferred. The plan marks it optional, and a map tile provider is a vendor and privacy decision. It's added to `docs/DECISIONS.md` as an open question and not built here.
- **Results.**
  - Cards show avatar, name, headline, city, categories, rating and "from" price (`startingPrice`, formatted through `lib/money.ts`), and link to the profile.
  - Pagination is cursor-based: a "load more" link with `?cursor=` keeps it crawlable without JS, with no infinite scroll.
  - Empty state: a message plus a "clear filters" link.
- **Filters UI.**
  - A plain `<form method="get">` with city autocomplete (client component, debounced 250 ms, calling `/v1/cities`), category, language and price min/max in whole currency units, converted to cents.
  - It works without JS; the autocomplete is progressive.
  - Invalid params are dropped, never a 400 page: they're validated with a web-side schema derived from `PhotographerSearchQuerySchema`.
- **Landing pages.**
  - ISR `revalidate = 3600`.
  - H1 and intro from i18n with ICU plurals, for example "{count, plural, …} wedding photographers in Luxembourg".
  - The first page of results is server-rendered, plus links to sibling categories and top cities (internal linking).
  - Metadata: title, description, canonical and hreflang with x-default, plus `ItemList` JSON-LD of the listed profiles (escaped, CSP nonce, reusing the 1B.3 serialiser).
  - Pages with zero results render but are `noindex`, to avoid thin content.
  - Indexing stays governed by `NEXT_PUBLIC_ALLOW_INDEXING` (the preview is noindex).
- **Header nav.** "Find a photographer" links to `/{locale}/photographers`.
- **Sitemap** entries for landing pages are 1B.11.

## 1B.4a tasks (api-developer)

1. `packages/shared`:
   - move the profile slugify into `src/slug.ts` with tests, used by the API's `slug.ts`;
   - contract `cities.ts` with `CitySummarySchema` and the route;
   - `countryCode` added to `PhotographerSearchQuerySchema`;
   - regenerate openapi and api-client.
2. `apps/api`:
   - `CitiesController` and service: parameterised Prisma raw SQL, `unaccent` only if the extension already exists, otherwise normalise in TS over the grouped result, which is bounded by distinct cities;
   - search repository `countryCode` condition;
   - a 2-letter slug test.
3. Integration tests:
   - only published profiles count;
   - prefix match is case- and accent-insensitive;
   - `limit` bound;
   - `countryCode` filter;
   - `Cache-Control` header.

## 1B.4b tasks (web-developer)

1. Routes above:
   - `lib/search-params.ts` (parse and serialise, drop invalid, round coordinates) with tests;
   - `components/photographer-card.tsx`, `search-filters.tsx`, `city-autocomplete.tsx` and `near-me-button.tsx`.
2. The `[slug]` dispatch and nested landing pages, with metadata and `ItemList` JSON-LD.
3. i18n keys under `web.search.*` and `web.landing.*` in `en`; i18n-maintainer drafts the other locales.
4. Tests:
   - search params parsing;
   - country, city and category 404s;
   - a 2-letter segment routes to the landing and a longer one to the profile;
   - noindex when filtered or with zero results;
   - `ItemList` shape and escaping;
   - the autocomplete debounces and handles API errors without breaking the form.
   - Web coverage must not drop.
5. Manual check against the local stack with seed data: `/en/photographers?category=wedding`, `/en/photographers/lu` and `/en/photographers/lu/luxembourg/wedding`.
